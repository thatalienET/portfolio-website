Overview

As part of a collaboration with brand designer Skojie, I created a collection of motion assets for the concept brand Phantom Pops. We were looking for a wany to stand out against other candy brands, and so devised a stratagy that made easy for both kids and young adults to connect with the brand. Childeren, through the interactive animation which encourages play and a deeper connection to the flavour mascots. Young Adults through social formats, and overall nostalgic retro aesthetic in the TV ad.
